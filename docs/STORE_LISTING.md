**Name:** The Narrows

**Short line:** Drive the South Coast, from Fall River to Provincetown.

**Description:**

The Narrows is an open-world driving game on the real South Coast of Massachusetts. Walk the Quequechan, drive I-195, Route 24, US 6, and the canal bridges, and take the chapters through Fall River, New Bedford, Brockton, the towns between, and Cape Cod.

Two ways to play the same coast. The street map is built from OpenStreetMap. The other page uses Google Photorealistic 3D Tiles when the host has a Map Tiles key. Fares, street races, and a discovery log (Lizzie Borden House, the Braga Bridge, the Whaling Museum, the Rocky Marciano statue, the canal bridges, Provincetown) are in both.

**Platform:** Browser. A phone uses the on-screen stick. No native package is produced by this repository.

**Age and rating:** Not rated. A person has to pick the rating board and answer the questionnaire. The game has driving, a police chase, and a fist fight. It has no music tracks in the download.
